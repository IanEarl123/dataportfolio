# About Me

I’m Ian Earl, a data professional passionate about transforming raw information into actionable insights. I specialize in cleaning, organizing, and analyzing datasets to uncover meaningful patterns that support smarter decision-making. With strong attention to detail and a problem-solving mindset, I thrive on turning complex data into clear, impactful stories that drive business success.

I also focus on improving efficiency in my work by automating about 30% of my routine tasks. This includes generating letters for merchandisers, setting up reminders, and managing renewal processes. By combining data expertise with workflow automation, I’m able to deliver results that are both accurate and timely, while freeing up more time to focus on strategic analysis and innovation.
